Project overview

Turning research results into innovation through Artificial Intelligence

Technology Transfer is the process through which scientific and technological research is transformed into solutions, services and products able to generate economic and social value. In healthcare, this process is particularly critical because scientific innovation is rapid, highly specialised and closely connected to intellectual property, regulation and market dynamics.

TIDE addresses this challenge by introducing an engineered, data-driven approach to Technology Transfer. The project aims to build an advanced hardware and software infrastructure able to collect and analyse heterogeneous data, evaluate different transfer strategies, estimate the value of research results and support decision-makers through Artificial Intelligence.

Although the methods developed are general-purpose, the project focuses specifically on the Digital Health domain and on the innovation ecosystem promoted by DARE.

The challenge

Bridging the innovation “valley of death”

01

Fragmented evidence

Technology Transfer decisions often rely on heterogeneous information distributed across patents, scientific outputs, markets and organisational contexts.

02

Limited benchmark data

Dedicated datasets for training and evaluating AI algorithms specifically designed for Technology Transfer are still scarce.

03

Decision complexity

IP protection, licensing, partnerships and commercialisation require the joint assessment of scientific, technological, legal and economic factors.

04

Need for explainability

AI-based recommendations must be interpretable and transparent before they can effectively support strategic Technology Transfer decisions.

Research goals

From data to intelligent Technology Transfer strategies

Goal 01

Build the technological infrastructure

Design and deploy an AI/HPC infrastructure capable of supporting data-intensive and GPU-accelerated workloads for Technology Transfer analytics.

Goal 02

Create benchmark data

Define acquisition protocols and build datasets suitable for training and evaluating data-driven models, enriched with synthetic data when necessary.

Goal 03

Develop an AI algorithm suite

Create predictive and decision-support models for identifying promising Technology Transfer strategies in healthcare and pharmaceutical contexts.

Goal 04

Enable explainable decision support

Integrate Explainable AI approaches that combine predictive performance with clear and actionable explanations for decision-makers.

Goal 05

Validate on heterogeneous use cases

Test the effectiveness of the proposed models across different Technology Transfer processes, organisational characteristics and Digital Health scenarios.

Goal 06

Open the ecosystem to stakeholders

Make data, algorithms and research outputs reusable by the DARE community, academia, Technology Transfer Offices and the productive sector.

Methodology

An iterative AI-enabled Technology Transfer pipeline

TIDE adopts an Agile, iterative and incremental methodology. Requirements, data models, AI components and decision-support functionalities are progressively refined through frequent releases, rapid testing and continuous interaction with domain experts and stakeholders.

01

Data acquisition

Collect heterogeneous data from scientific, technological, IP and innovation-related sources.

02

Knowledge base

Structure benchmark data and integrate synthetic information when real-world evidence is insufficient.

03

AI & LLM analysis

Use Machine Learning, Large Language Models and advanced analytics to extract signals and predict promising strategies.

04

Explainability

Generate interpretable evidence supporting the rationale behind predictions and recommendations.

05

Technology Transfer decisions

Support IP protection, valorisation and transfer strategies in Digital Health contexts.
